GET TO KNOW US

A Church Built on Faith, Love and Unity

Latter Glory Wonders Chapel International is a Christian ministry committed to sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ, teaching the Word of God, nurturing spiritual growth, and demonstrating God's love through service.

Our desire is to see individuals and families grow in faith, develop strong Christian character, discover their God-given purpose, and become positive influences in their communities.

Our Vision

To raise a people who know God, walk in His truth, demonstrate His love, and become instruments of hope, transformation, and positive change in their generation.

Our Mission

To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ, teach God's Word, encourage prayer and fellowship, strengthen families, nurture spiritual maturity, and serve our communities with love and compassion.

Our Core Values

Faith • Love • Unity • Prayer • Integrity • Service
